Installed at runtime 🛍️

This plugin is not composed in the app's main.ts. It lives in the testbed's plugin catalog and you installed it from Settings → Plugin store — it spawned the moment you accepted the install dialog, without a reload.

Its permissions are exactly what that dialog listed (here: contribute to the UI, nothing else), and you stay in charge: revoke rights or uninstall it under Settings, and this tab disappears again — live. Same <iframe sandbox> isolation and default-deny broker as every sandboxed plugin.
